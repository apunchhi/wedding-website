2. Our Story 
Ok, forgive us… this one’s a little long.
Technically, we first met around 2010 at Quantum Park in Khar, Mumbai — although Amar doesn’t actually remember it.
Quantum Park was the kind of place where everyone seemed to know everyone. A group of moms had started a book club, and naturally, their similarly-aged children became friends too. Ananya and Amar’s middle sister, Gopika met this way and would occasionally hang out after school.
One day, Gopika invited Ananya over and Amar, being only a year older than us, approached us while playing with a Rubik’s cube. Gopika mentioned how her and Amar had been studying how to solve Rubik’s cubes, and they both taught Ananya how to solve them quickly. Amar actually doesn’t remember this story (LOL), but Ananya has a very vivid memory of it because it sparked a months-long obsession with watching speed Rubik’s cubing on Youtube. That was pretty much the only memory she has of Amar but it stuck. By all intents and purposes though, we weren’t friends. 
After 2010, our families continued moving around. Amar’s family left that building in 2011, stayed in Mumbai until 2014 before heading over to Hong Kong and settling back in Canada.  Ananya’s family moved to NYC in 2012 and has been in the city ever since. But over 10 years, it was our Moms who stayed in touch. In fact, all the 10-11 Moms from that book club in Quantum Park somehow remained good friends despite many of them moving to different parts of the world. Ananya, Gopika, Amar all lost touch as we moved through different cities and phases, but we still peripherally knew of each other's existence. 
Ananya’s good friend from Bombay, Ameera, was throwing a party for her dad’s birthday. Ameera had gone to school with Gopika and Amar, her older brother Eshaan was Amar’s best friend in high school, and her mom was also part of the Quantum Park book club crew.
Naturally, Amar and Ananya were both invited.
After more than a decade, we reconnected that night and immediately hit it off. We spent most of the evening talking… but somehow never exchanged numbers.
A few days later, Amar texted Ameera to ask if Ananya was dating anyone.
“She’s not… I could give you her number if you’d like to ask her out?”
But Amar, not wanting to be too forward with a family friend, replied:
“You know what… it’s okay. I’ll just leave this one up to fate.”
And apparently, fate had other plans.
Eight months went by. We didn’t speak, but both of us admitted later that we thought about each other from time to time.
Then, in February 2023, Ananya went out with a couple of girlfriends to support her friend Ariana’s event at a bar in New York. Completely independently, Amar happened to take two friends who were visiting him to that exact same bar.
In a sea of people, music, and LED lights, we ran into each other again.
This time, we decided not to leave anything up to fate.
We went on our first date a few days later, started dating on March 19, 2023, and the rest, as they say, is history.
