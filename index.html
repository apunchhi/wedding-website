Our Story

We met in 2010 when we both lived in the same apartment building in Mumbai: Quantum Park.

Amar’s sister, Gopika and Ananya would often hang out after school. On one particular afternoon, Amar barged into Gopika’s room to share his recent obsession with Rubik’s cubes. While Gopika was mortified by her brother’s presence, Ananya couldn’t help but think that the curly-haired boy, sharing his love for puzzles, was incredibly cute.

In the following months, Ananya developed her own obsession with speed cubing that crystallized her first impression of Amar. Amar, far too obsessed with shaving off seconds from his solve time, would fail to remember the day he met his future wife.

Over the next decade, the Punchhi and Tinaikar families would move around the world. Our moms continued to stay in touch, but we never crossed paths.

Fast forward to July 2022, NYC. A birthday party for our friend Ameera’s Dad, Salim, brought us together again. Ananya reminded Amar that they had met before, and we proceeded to spend several hours talking. Despite this promising start, we still failed to exchange numbers.

This time, Amar would make sure to remember their meeting.

He texted Ameera, who offered to pass along Ananya’s number. But, not wanting to be too forward with a family friend, Amar responded:

“It’s okay. I’ll just leave this one up to fate.”

In February 2023, Ananya attended a @drinksfirst event hosted by her friend, Ariana. By coincidence, Amar decided to go out with his friends to that very same event.

In a sea of people, music, and LED lights, we ran into each other…again! We finally exchanged numbers and the rest is history.

In November 2025, Amar proposed to Ananya on the rooftop of Quantum Park, and after many years, fate finally got its way.

Chandigarh in November

November is one of the best times to be in Chandigarh. Days are sunny and pleasant; evenings drop enough that you’ll want a layer.

Days

Mid-to-high 70s°F (24–27°C). Bring a hat or sunglasses if you’re outdoors.

Evenings

Low-to-mid 50s°F (10–13°C). Bring a light jacket or shawl for after dark.

Rain

November is typically the driest month. An umbrella is optional, not a strategy.

Air

November can get hazy from crop burning. If you’re pregnant or have asthma, bring a mask.

November climatology: Climate-Data.org and Weather & Climate.

Do I need to book my own hotel?

No. Our families are covering the hotel for November 19th and 20th — you’ll be at Hyatt Centric, Sector 17 . Send your RSVP and flight details to the wedding planners; they’ll share booking info closer to the date and arrange airport pick-up and drop-off.

What will the weather be like?

November is one of the best times to be in Chandigarh! Expect sunny, pleasant days in the mid-to-high 70s°F (24–27°C), with cooler evenings in the low-to-mid 50s°F (10–13°C). If you tend to run cold, we recommend bringing a light jacket or shawl for the evenings!

Are plus-ones and/or additional guests allowed?

Unfortunately, we’re unable to accommodate plus-ones or additional guests unless they are specifically named on your invitation.

What are the visa requirements for visiting India?

Indian passport: nothing extra. Valid OCI card: bring it with your current foreign passport. Most other guests should apply for an e-Tourist Visa on the official site only — indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa — at least four days before travel (sooner is better). You’ll need six months’ passport validity, two blank pages, a return ticket, and a GRANTED ETA printout. Chandigarh airport is a designated e-Visa entry point.

Pakistani origin or a Pakistani passport: e-Visa is not available; apply for a regular visa at the Indian mission. Minors need their own passport and application. Ignore third parties offering “express” e-Visas.
